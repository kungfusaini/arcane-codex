What makes a human piece of art valuable? 

It's a question I was considering with my partner this weekend.

We could say that it's the amount of thought that goes into an art piece. An artist has a thousand references, thoughts and their own taste within their subconcious mind. Every single one of these affects the final mark the place on a page. A painting is a combination of these strokes, each one a grain of sand that becomes a beach. Sometimes we make a stroke thoughtlessly, but nothing is every truly created in a vaccuum. 

But isn't that kinda the same with AI. A gen AI model has been fed a countless number of images, thoughts on humanity, and read more prose than the most expressive writer. We can't say for sure which ideas come from where, but because it's only trained on himan data, it can only learn from human ideas. 

So, what sets us apart? I think a big part that's being left out is execution. Imagine an artist having an idea to paint a sunset view. The artist will get a flash in their mind, a glimpse of what thier painting could be like. Now image if he could transfer his mind instantly to the canvas. I think this art would probably be quite bad... No thought has gone into the actual construction of the peice. If he had to paint it by hand, eveything would have to be considered in 100x more detail. Everyblade of grass will be drawn. If it looks bad, the artist might adjust thier grip, and continue. What might have been imagined as a bright sun in the sky, becomes layers and layers and layers of iterative work, with so much consideration into the brightness, warmth and overall feeling on the sun. 

With generative AI, we are effetvley asking an artist "what do you image a sunset to be", and then taking the first image they see in their mind and passing that off as the final image! Perhaps it's time to program in some execution ?
